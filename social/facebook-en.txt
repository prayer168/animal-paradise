🎲 What happens when a property-trading board game meets wildlife conservation?

Meet Animal Kingdom Tycoon, a new browser game from Black Bear Teacher’s Nature Classroom! 🐻🐰

Roll the dice, explore six habitats, manage animal reserves, upgrade conservation facilities, and tackle science questions. Four new features add more choices to every game:

🎯 Secret missions: Two goals per player, with cash rewards for completing them.
🤝 Land trading and auctions: Negotiate deals and compete for your next reserve.
✨ Active character abilities: Six animal characters, each with a special power.
⚡ A 10-round option: Choose a shorter game when you want a quick challenge.

👥 Play solo against computer opponents, or take turns with 2–6 players on one device.
🌿 Designed for upper-elementary learners, family play, and after-class fun.
💻 No installation needed—play in your browser with an internet connection.
🗣️ The game interface and science questions are in Traditional Chinese.

Will you save your cash, complete a habitat, or chase a secret mission? Try it and tell me your favorite character! 👇
